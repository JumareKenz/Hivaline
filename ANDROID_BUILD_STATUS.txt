🔐 SIGNING & UPLOADING APK:

  Step 1: Create Keystore (one-time)
    keytool -genkey -v -keystore ~/hiva-release-key.keystore \
      -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000 -alias hiva-key-alias

  Step 2: Sign APK
    jarsigner -verbose -sigalg SHA256withRSA -digestalg SHA-256 \
      -keystore ~/hiva-release-key.keystore \
      android/app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release-unsigned.apk \
      hiva-key-alias

  Step 3: Align APK
    zipalign -v 4 app-release-unsigned.apk app-release-signed.apk

  Step 4: Upload to Google Play Console
    Settings → Release → Production → Upload app-release-signed.apk
